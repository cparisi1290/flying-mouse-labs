Who We Are

We're five military wives who met while working together training emergency room trauma staff. Those experiences were challenging but rewarding. We're disciplined and process-oriented with wisdom and camaraderie forged in the high-stress trenches of ER life.

Why We Do What We Do

Most people spend their working lives as employees, which makes sense. It's a straightforward path where decisions are made for you. But employees are always boxed in, limiting their personal freedom and their financial indepedence. Small business owners are different. They had the creativity to become their own bosses, the guts to bet on themselves and take destiny into their own hands. Here at Silver Tree, we admire that deeply. But it's hard for entrepreneurs to wear all hats all at once. It overwhelms a lot of business owners. That's why we're here. We want to see small business owners achieve their goals and enjoy the success they've always dreamed of.

What We Do

We take all of the knowledge, skills and tools we learned from our years of real experience and use them to help entrepreneurs level up their businesses. We offer process-oriented business consulting and services including staff recruiting and virtual assistance services. We also host custom webinars and workshops to help your company learn and grow.

Core Values

Serving others

We believe deeply in service. We serve our customers so they can better serve their communities.

Educating entrepreneurs

Many business owners have amazing products and services. We believe in building systems that support their success.

Making ourselves obsolete

We think of our service like a bootcamp. Our goal is for our customers to graduate and become confidently self-sufficient.
